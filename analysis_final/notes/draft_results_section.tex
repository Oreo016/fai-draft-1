\section{Empirical Evaluation}
\label{sec:results}

We evaluate our receiver-aware Value of Communication ($\mathrm{VoC}$) framework on the heterogeneous Search-and-Rescue Dec-POMDP. The environment consists of a $10\times 10$ grid populated by 3 victims, 2 hazards, and a 3-agent heterogeneous team (Scout, Carrier, Medic). Hazard dynamics follow move probability $p_{\mathrm{move}}=0.8$ and toggle probability $p_{\mathrm{toggle}}=0.2$. Rollout valuation employs $N_p=64$ particles, shortlist size 8, and $N_{\mathrm{roll}}=32$ rollouts with Common Random Numbers (CRN), evaluated across 10 evaluation seeds (0--9) over an episode horizon of $T=100$.

\subsection{Hypothesis Testing and Core Findings}
Table~\ref{tab:hypothesis_summary} summarizes the pre-specified targets, observed values, 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals, and Holm-adjusted $p$-values across hypotheses H1--H4.

\textbf{H1: Receiver Differentiation.} Receiver differentiation requires that candidate receivers exhibit substantially different marginal values ($|\mathrm{MV}(m, j_1) - \mathrm{MV}(m, j_2)| > \tau = 0.5$) in at least 30\% of matched contexts. In our primary evaluation, we observe a differentiation fraction of $0.2857$ (95\% cluster CI: $[0.0196, 0.5574]$, 95\% context CI: $[0.1633, 0.4082]$). Although this fraction misses the $0.30$ pre-specified threshold by $0.0143$, Holm-adjusted hypothesis testing on held-out contexts confirms significant differentiation over zero ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.0148$). Furthermore, the aware model exceeds the role-only baseline differentiation rate of $0.2000$ by $+0.0857$, demonstrating that belief-conditioned state estimation adds value beyond static role distinctions.

\textbf{H2: Selectivity and Communication Reduction.} Hypothesis H2 posits that receiver-aware communication achieves a communication reduction $\mathrm{CRB} \ge 0.20$ relative to full broadcast while maintaining matched task return. In full-episode evaluations, aware communication at $\lambda=0.0$ achieves a mean discounted return of $J_0 = 3.436$ with $N_{\mathrm{msg}} = 12.6$, outperforming full broadcast ($J_0 = 3.330, N_{\mathrm{msg}} = 87.6$) and achieving an $85.72\%$ communication reduction at matched return ($\mathrm{CRB} = 0.8572$, exceeding the $0.20$ threshold). The paired return difference over agnostic communication across all episodes is $+0.5724$ (95\% CI: $[0.1615, 1.1027]$). However, when evaluated under strict single-context budget matching in E08, the return difference narrows to $-0.1792$ (95\% CI: $[-0.5817, 0.0443]$, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 1.0000$), indicating that the benefits of selectivity stem from global trajectory pruning rather than isolated token swaps.

\textbf{H3: Downstream Value of Nonmyopic Valuation.} Nonmyopic valuation ($H_{\mathrm{NM}}=5$) achieved oracle decision agreement of $\mathrm{Agree} = 0.8100$, compared to $\mathrm{Agree} = 0.7700$ for myopic valuation ($H_{\mathrm{myo}}=1$), yielding an agreement gain of $+0.0400$ (95\% CI: $[-0.2000, 0.3200]$). This falls short of the pre-specified target of $+0.1000$. Analysis of delayed-value contexts in study E08 revealed that only 3 out of 10 seeds produced contexts where oracle valuations differed across horizons, yielding zero delayed agreement gain ($0.0000, p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 1.0000$). Under fast hazard dynamics ($p_{\mathrm{move}}=0.8$), spatial coordination signals rapidly decay, limiting the downstream utility of multi-step rollouts.

\textbf{H4: Cost-Aware Communication Throttling.} In accordance with H4, scaling communication cost weight from $\lambda=0.0$ to $\lambda=0.5$ reduced the positive-$\mathrm{VoC}$ communication rate from $0.1666$ to $0.0233$, representing an $86.00\%$ relative reduction (95\% CI: $[0.6488, 0.9054]$) and substantially exceeding the $0.1500$ threshold. In null calibration studies (E06), strict $\mathrm{VoC} > 0$ selection produced a $31.10\%$ false-positive transmission rate on redundant messages, whereas Lower Confidence Bound (LCB) selection reduced false positives to $0.30\%$ while preserving a $100.0\%$ rate drop under communication cost.

\subsection{Baseline Comparison and Performance Frontier}
Table~\ref{tab:baseline_comparison} compares the receiver-aware framework against seven baselines. Aware nonmyopic communication achieves the highest overall task return ($J_0 = 3.436$) while leaving the lowest number of unrescued victims ($0.90$). In contrast, role-only routing sends $1.000$ messages per context to achieve $J_0 = 2.459$, while myopic valuation completely fails to communicate ($N_{\mathrm{msg}} = 0.000, J_0 = 2.336$), demonstrating the necessity of nonmyopic valuation. Figure~\ref{fig:pareto_frontier} illustrates the Pareto frontier across cost weights $\lambda \in [0.0, 1.0]$. As $\lambda$ increases from $0.0$ to $0.5$, aware communication prunes noisy candidate messages, driving return from $J_0 = 1.658$ to $J_0 = 2.474$ at $N_{\mathrm{msg}} = 0.10$.

\subsection{Ablation Synthesis}
Table~\ref{tab:ablations} details ablations across belief particles, rollout count, and continuation assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Particle Diagnostics (E03)}: Increasing particle count from $N_p=16$ to $N_p=256$ demonstrates a sharp reduction in pair regret at $N_p=64$ (dropping from $0.5405$ to $0.2417$), validating the default filter size. Effective sample size remained $1.000$ across all configurations.
    \item \textbf{Variance Reduction via CRN (E04)}: At $N_{\mathrm{roll}}=32$, Common Random Numbers reduced estimator standard error by $27.0\%$ ($0.0817$ vs $0.1119$, variance reduction $46.6\%$) with negligible bias ($-0.0199$).
    \item \textbf{Continuation Bias (E05)}: Disabling communication in future rollouts exhibited $100.0\%$ top-1 policy agreement and rank correlation $\rho = 0.8381$ against the communication-disabled oracle, with low absolute error ($\mathrm{MAE} = 0.0230$).
    \item \textbf{Staleness Validation (E10)}: Observed marginal values for hazard messages were identically $0.0000$, whereas fresh victim discoveries achieved values up to $2.0199$ (Figure~\ref{fig:mv_vs_info_age}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Robustness and Runtime Profiles}
Across environmental variations (Table~\ref{tab:robustness} and Figure~\ref{fig:robustness_bars}), aware communication maintains stable performance on larger grids ($12\times 12$ return difference $0.0000$), while reducing hazard dynamics from $p=0.8$ to $p=0.2$ narrows the paired performance deficit from $-1.0847$ to $-0.5786$. Profiling in study E12 confirms that rollout estimation accounts for $71.6\%$--$81.3\%$ of execution time ($0.104\text{s}$--$0.127\text{s}$ per decision), the oracle requires $18.6\%$--$24.3\%$ ($0.233\text{s}$--$0.309\text{s}$), and particle filter updates require $9.8\%$--$11.2\%$ ($0.014\text{s}$--$0.017\text{s}$).
